# Active rules in the playable edition

[Português](validacao-da-edicao.md) · **English**

The 231 levels are canonical level records. The tide turns every three valid steps. The campaign uses shallows, solid refuges, pearls, oysters, shells, the heron, fissures, and simultaneous movement of the Siris. In team play, cells occupied at the start of a turn are treated as blocked when moving.

The cleanup removed maps, engine support, and drawings for discarded historical mechanisms from the playable source. It preserved the previous edition's IDs and ordering, including old IDs that appear only as save-compatibility text. Nautical scenery elements and foam remain decorative, with no damage or turn events.

The automated test replays solutions, checks the tide, and requires the current campaign's map-symbol alphabet and fields. It makes no claims about perceived difficulty.
