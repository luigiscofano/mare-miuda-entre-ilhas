## Errors, recovery, and results

The engine continues to count steps and errors normally. After a recoverable sweep-away event caused by the tide or heron, the campaign interface shows the Siri returning to the last solid foothold and discards directional input during the pause; this interval neither consumes a step nor queues actions. The affected Siri blinks three times during recovery, except with reduced motion. The third error ends the attempt. These effects change no map, objective, unlock, or save.

The [interface guide](../interface/README.en.md#results-and-recovery) explains recovery and reduced-motion behavior. Standalone levels from [Ilha de Improviso](../interface/README.en.md#improvisation-island) retain their own rules and do not alter campaign progression.
