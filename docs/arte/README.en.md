## Ocean in the level selectors

The corners use the original sand art for each page. Small dots and three sizes of cross-shaped glints match each world's palette. The detached foam crests cycle through five pixel-art poses: short, flat arms shift a few pixels without interpolation. These details appear only in open water on each world's four pages; they do not cover titles, routes, cards, sand, or rocks.

The [level selector](../interface/README.en.md#level-selection) respects the reduced-motion preference by showing a single still frame.
