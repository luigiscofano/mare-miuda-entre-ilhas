Shell Beach adds [a soft landing sound](../../../edicao-oficial/recursos/passo-areia.wav) and adjusts the timing and balance of the tide and outcome sounds. The MP3 predates that change.

The catalog presents the sounds used in the offline edition one by one. The effects have softer attacks, and the click is a short mechanical sound. All use the **Efeitos** (Effects) volume, separate from **Música** (Music) in **Configurações** (Settings). The nylon-string guitar track **A Outra Margem** is unchanged.

| Approx. time in the MP3 | Effect | Trigger |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 00:00 | Rising tide | When the incoming tide covers the flats. |
| 00:02 | Falling tide | When the outgoing tide uncovers the flats. |
| 00:04 | Pearl | When a pearl is collected. |
| 00:06 | Victory | When a level is completed. |
| 00:08 | Digging | When a crab starts entering a burrow. |
| 00:10 | Burrow exit | When a crab starts emerging from the other end. |
| 00:12 | Swept away | When water reaches a crab in an unsafe position. |
| 00:14 | Reappearance | When a crab reappears after a recoverable mistake. |
| 00:16 | Heron | At the moment the dive strikes. |
| 00:18 | Defeat | As the result begins to appear, after the hazard. |
| 00:20 | Button click | Once per activation of an enabled button by mouse, touch, or keyboard. No sound on hover, focus, or a blocked activation. |

The MP3 is a reference for listening. The game plays individual sound files as their events occur. Alerts, changes on the board, and text convey the same information when playing without sound.

[The Eight Worlds film](../animacoes/README.en.md#the-eight-worlds) also uses these sounds in its replay edit.
